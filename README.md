# Weather-Checking-Habits-Analysis-Across-U.S.-Regions

## Project Overview
This project analyzes weather-checking habits across different regions in the United States. The goal is to identify behavioral trends, understand weather engagement patterns, and generate business insights for smart wearable companies and government agencies. 

## Business Problem
The company wants to:

1.Understand which regions check weather updates most frequently\
2.Identify potential markets for smartwatches and weather-enabled devices\
3.Identify most used weather channel among regions to improve targeted advertising campaigns\
4.Help government agencies understand weather-awareness behavior\

## Data Cleaning
1.Removed duplicate records\
2.Handled missing values\
3.Grouped Age into groups\
4.Converted date formats\

## Analysis
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
The analysis focused on:
1. Regions with highest weather-check frequency
2. Age groups most engaged with weather apps
3. Weather checking methods usage patterns across regions
4. Relationship between weather awareness and smartwatch ownership

## Key Insights
1. New England and East South central regions checked weather updates more frequently while West North central and pacific has lower
2. mobile devices (Weather app on phone, Internet search, the weather chennal app and specific apps or website) accounted for over 72% of weather checks  <img width="309" height="335" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d88c81a1-6995-4f48-ad7e-f22a0172ebd4" />
3. Users in Age group 45-59, and 18-25 showed higher smartwatch adoption and are more likely to check weather with a smartwatch
4. Individuals between the age group 18-29 and 30- 44 preferred checking weather updates on their phones more as compared to 45-60+ who perferred TV
